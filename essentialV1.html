Bluqar Houseboat operates around the Royal Belum / Pulau Banding area in Perak. Exact meeting and boarding instructions can be provided when your booking is confirmed.

Bluqar can cater to houseboat escapes and nature experiences for families, friends and groups. Contact the team with your preferred date and group size for the most suitable option.

Houseboat capacity and sleeping arrangements should be confirmed directly with Bluqar based on your preferred trip and group requirements.

Inclusions may depend on the package selected. Ask the Bluqar team for the latest package inclusions, activities and meal arrangements before confirming your trip.

Use the enquiry form below or WhatsApp Bluqar directly with your preferred date, number of guests and trip duration.
